About Meridian Logistics

Meridian Logistics was founded to give small and mid-size manufacturers the same delivery reliability that larger companies get from national carriers, without the overhead of managing in-house fleets or warehouses.

We operate three regional distribution centres and a partner carrier network covering last-mile delivery within a 400 km radius of each site.

Our values

Reliability

We quote lead times we can keep, and we tell customers early when a shipment is at risk.

Transparency

Account teams get the same tracking data our dispatchers see, not a summarised version.

Continuous improvement

Routes and warehouse layouts are reviewed quarterly against delivery and cost data.
